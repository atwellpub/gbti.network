Kotaku reports that recent GTA 6 gameplay leaks have revealed at least 14 apparent radio stations and more than 30 songs spanning classic rock, country, pop, hip-hop, Latin music, reggae, electronic music, and classical compositions. Returning Vice City stations such as V-Rock and Emotion 98.3 appear alongside new stations including Kaleidoscope FM, Worldwide FM, Dirty South Classics, Symphony FM, and The Chamber 106.6. Identified tracks range from Kansas’ “Carry On Wayward Son,” Joan Jett’s “I Love Rock ‘n’ Roll,” and George Strait’s “The Fireman” to newer songs from Tommy Richman, Tate McRae, Coi Leray, and Sexyy Red. The list combines music heard in leaked gameplay with official trailers, but Kotaku cautions that some tracks or stations could change before GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026.
