An entertaining video about the open source game Cataclysm, and how it was forked into Dark Days Ahead, which was then forked again into Bright Nights. Both are still played today, and they compete for the same players.

**The original**

Cataclysm by Whales, first committed in 2010 and now archived.

- Source: [github.com/Whales/Cataclysm](https://github.com/Whales/Cataclysm)

**Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead**

The main continuation, maintained by CleverRaven. It is free from the project site, and the Steam build is a paid convenience release.

- Site: [cataclysmdda.org](https://cataclysmdda.org)
- Source: [github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA)
- Discord: [discord.gg/jFEc7Yp](https://discord.gg/jFEc7Yp)
- Steam: [Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2330750/Cataclysm_Dark_Days_Ahead/)

**Cataclysm: Bright Nights**

A later fork of Dark Days Ahead that keeps a more sci-fi and less strictly realistic direction. There is no current Steam release.

- Docs: [docs.cataclysmbn.org](https://docs.cataclysmbn.org/)
- Source: [github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN](https://github.com/cataclysmbn/Cataclysm-BN)
- Discord: [discord.gg/XW7XhXuZ89](https://discord.gg/XW7XhXuZ89)
