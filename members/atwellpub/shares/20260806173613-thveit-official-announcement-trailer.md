Winter Cats Studios and publisher Crytivo announced Thveit on July 21, 2026, a storybook Norse city builder for PC in which a handful of settlers turn one corner of a procedurally generated island into a self-sustaining village. You raise homes, farms, workshops, docks and sacred structures across layered production chains, and because every season leaves its mark, most of the planning comes down to surviving the winter. What sets it apart is who you share the land with: folklore creatures are neighbors to study and live beside rather than enemies to clear out, and temples to the old gods earn divine favor. It arrives on Steam Early Access soon and is expected to stay in Early Access into late 2027.
