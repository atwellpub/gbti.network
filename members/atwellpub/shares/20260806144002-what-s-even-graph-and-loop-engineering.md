"People are starting to say that instead of just prompting AI agents, you should be writing loops and defining graphs. Sounds abstract, but these are legit engineering patterns, and you are probably already using them.

We cover:
why loops are more than repeated prompting
how success criteria change agent behavior
turn-based, goal-based, time-based, and proactive loops
why sub-agents already form graphs
parallel graphs, sequential graphs, and dynamic workflows
how LangGraph fits into the same idea
when to use a single-agent loop versus a multi-agent graph
why graphs can reduce context pollution in complex workflows
